Why Payments BA?

Payment exceptions and investigations sit where money, regulation and client trust meet — every recall, cancellation, non-receipt claim and approval threshold carries financial and compliance consequences. Over 5+ years at EvonSys I've moved from enterprise workflow platforms into Tier-1 payments, working on investigation programmes at JPMC, NatWest and now Goldman Sachs. I define how cases are created, routed and resolved across SWIFT MT, ISO 20022 / CBPR+ and domestic rails, which verification controls apply before funds move, and what correspondence goes back to counterparties — turning manual, spreadsheet-driven processes into structured, auditable case management.

Senior Business Analyst  ·  Transaction Banking (TxB), USA  ·  Pega Case Management

Enterprise Pega Payment Exceptions & Investigations (E&I) initiative consolidating fragmented processing into a single case-management model across multiple payment rails.

Requirements & Scope

  • Led requirements elicitation and business analysis for the E&I programme, supporting cross-border and correspondent-payment operations across multiple payment rails.
  • Authored and refined epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, functional requirements, business rules, use cases and process flows with operations, product and technology stakeholders.
  • Supported investigation expansion beyond SWIFT across Fedwire, CHAPS, SEPA CT/DD, StoneX and T2, alongside SWIFT MT and SWIFT MX / CBPR+ investigation processing.
  • Defined requirements across investigation categories: Payment Cancellation, Recall, Amendment, Tracer, Request for Information / Unable to Apply, Fraud, Regulatory and Sanctions / Compliance.

Case Lifecycle & Automation

  • Defined functional requirements for manual and automated case creation, investigation routing, orchestration, resolution activities, verification and approval controls, and outbound correspondence.
  • Phase 1 automation: keyword parsing, intent assignment, transaction lookup, client / transaction-data enrichment and case SLA monitoring.
  • Phase 2 capabilities: financial adjustments, payment-engine integration, foundational STP flows, workflow orchestration, real-time payment-status updates and enhanced case routing.

Messaging & Integration

  • SWIFT MT and ISO 20022 / CBPR+ message-processing requirements, including MT n9x and camt.056 / camt.029 / camt.110 / camt.111, with field mapping, validation and enrichment.
  • Integration requirements between Pega, payment orchestration, CRM, compliance / risk and customer / counterparty reference-data services.

Delivery

  • AS-IS / TO-BE and gap analysis translated into functional requirements, user stories and configuration / development requirements.
  • Facilitated backlog refinement and story walkthroughs; supported UAT, defect triage, traceability and business sign-off.
  • Coordinated Payment Clearing Operations (PCO), TxB Client Service, Corporate Treasury, Product and Operations with technology teams.

Impact

  • Contributed to transforming manual, Excel-based, fragmented E&I processes into a unified end-to-end Pega case-management model.
  • Supported Phase 2A Fedwire delivery through requirements clarification, UAT and defect triage — 13/13 user stories achieved UAT sign-off.

NatWest — Cross-Border Payment Investigations

Senior Business Analyst  ·  Pega Smart Investigate (Pega Infinity '24)  ·  UK

Cross-border payment-investigation implementation on Pega Smart Investigate covering intake through resolution and outbound correspondence.

Intake & Workflows

  • Defined end-to-end intake and case-creation requirements across front-office, back-office payment operations, automated inbound SWIFT MT / ISO 20022 messages and NLP-driven email channels.
  • Defined intent-to-resolution workflows for non-receipt claims, recalls / cancellations, return-to-sender, payment re-issue and requests for information.
  • Defined case routing, orchestration, resolution activities, outbound correspondence and exception handling from initiation through closure.

SWIFT MT & ISO 20022 Correspondence

  • Outbound correspondence requirements across SWIFT MT103, MT192 and MT199, plus ISO 20022 / CBPR+ pacs.008, pacs.004, camt.056, camt.110 and camt.029.
  • SWIFT Case Management and Stop-and-Recall requirements, including cancellation-reason handling for duplicate payment, fraud, incorrect amount and incorrect agent.

Controls, Detection & Reconciliation

  • 4-eye and 6-eye verification with Supervisor and Manager approval based on payment action and amount thresholds before funds movement or correspondence.
  • Duplicate-payment detection model using amount, date, transaction references and account information to drive automated linking vs. manual review.
  • Nostro / vostro reconciliation requirements across five break types, plus REST API integration between the reconciliation engine and the investigation platform.
  • Role-based access and persona functionality for Agent, Payment Specialist, Supervisor and Manager users.

Impact

  • Supported moving manual investigation activity into structured Pega Smart Investigate workflows covering intake, investigation, verification, resolution and correspondence.
  • Identified payment-processing limitations through AS-IS / TO-BE and gap analysis ahead of go-live, so required development and configuration changes were built into the target solution.
  • Led / supported UAT, defect triage and business sign-off.
