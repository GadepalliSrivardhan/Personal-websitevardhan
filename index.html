About

    

      
When a cross-border payment goes wrong, operations teams need a case that already knows which payment it is, which message to send and who has to approve it. I write the requirements that make that happen.

      
I specialise in cross-border and correspondent payments, payment exceptions and investigations, SWIFT MT, ISO 20022 / CBPR+ and Pega-based case management. My work covers intake and case creation, routing and orchestration, verification and approval controls, and outbound correspondence, from epics and user stories through UAT and business sign-off.

      
I'm a Certified Pega Business Architect and a Professional Scrum Product Owner, and I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at JNTU Kakinada.
